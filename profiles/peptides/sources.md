# Peptide source plan

Recommended knowledge buckets:
- **Clinical Science:** papers, trial reports, reviews and investigator interviews.
- **Mechanisms & Preclinical:** pharmacology and translational research.
- **Regulatory & Safety:** FDA/EMA/other official documents and safety communications.
- **Practitioners:** long-form interviews; label as interpretation.
- **Community:** podcasts/forums/transcripts only for reported experiences and hypothesis generation.

Every imported item should retain publication/upload date. For fast-moving regulatory questions, supplement NotebookLM with current official sources before concluding.
