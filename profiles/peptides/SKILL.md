# Peptide Research

Use the shared NotebookLM engine. This profile is research-first, not guru-first.

## Required separation
Never collapse these into one evidence bucket:
1. regulatory/official material;
2. peer-reviewed human clinical evidence;
3. preclinical animal/in-vitro evidence;
4. expert/practitioner interpretation;
5. community/anecdotal reports.

## Default analysis
For each compound or claim report:
- compound/target and proposed mechanism;
- approved/clinical/research status and date;
- strongest human evidence;
- preclinical evidence;
- safety/adverse-event evidence;
- what remains unknown;
- expert claims that go beyond the evidence;
- citations to the underlying sources.

Do not convert anecdotes into efficacy claims. When sources conflict, show the conflict and explain the evidence level.

## Notebook architecture
Use separate notebooks for Clinical Science, Mechanisms/Preclinical, Regulatory/Safety, Practitioner Discussion, and Community/Anecdotal material. Cross-query only after the evidence classes are identified.
