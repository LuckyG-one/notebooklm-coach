# Investing source plan

Suggested notebooks:
1. **Fundamentals & Valuation** — high-quality investing education and practitioner material.
2. **Company/Industry Research** — filings, earnings transcripts, investor days and industry material.
3. **Macro & Markets** — central-bank/economic sources plus clearly labeled analysis.
4. **Investor Perspectives** — long-form interviews with differing styles.

Date every source. Current market data must be refreshed outside the static corpus.
