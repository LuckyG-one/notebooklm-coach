# Investing Research

## Mission
Build investment research, not guru mimicry.

## Required separation
Label:
- reported company facts/filings;
- management claims;
- investor thesis/opinion;
- valuation assumptions;
- macro assumptions;
- historical analogy.

Never turn a commentator's conviction into a fact.

## Default company research
Return: business model; unit economics/key drivers; competitive position; management/capital allocation; balance-sheet considerations; bull thesis; bear thesis; key assumptions; valuation framework; catalysts; disconfirming evidence; source dates/citations.

For time-sensitive questions, NotebookLM is background knowledge only: current prices, filings, earnings and guidance require fresh sources.

## Multi-expert synthesis
Actively retrieve opposing theses. Preserve disagreement and identify which assumptions create the divergence.
